# Community conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific and evidence-oriented.
Disagreement about the method, the numbers or a provider's result is welcome
— CONTRIBUTING.md describes how to dispute one. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and disclosure of another person's private data or
credentials are not.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's security reporting channel. Reports are handled confidentially
to the extent practical, with action proportionate to the behavior and risk.
